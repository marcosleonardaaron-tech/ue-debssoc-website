01 — ABOUT THE SOCIETYVINCIT VERBUM

WHERE REASON
FINDS ITS VOICE.

The UE Debate and Speech Society is a recognized student organization at the University of the East – Manila dedicated to the power of thoughtful expression. We bring together individuals who believe that arguments should be grounded in reason, ideas should be challenged with respect, and every voice can create meaningful change.

Through debate, oratory, workshops, and collaborative activities, we nurture confidence, critical thinking, and a lifelong appreciation for the spoken word.
